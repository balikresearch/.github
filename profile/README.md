# Balik Research

**Research at the edge of silicon and intelligence.**

Balik Research is an independent research laboratory based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, exploring the intersection of applied artificial intelligence, hardware security, embedded systems, and experimental data science.

## Research Areas

**Applied Artificial Intelligence**  
Domain-specific AI, machine learning, intelligent agents, and AI-assisted engineering research.

**Hardware & Embedded Security**  
Security research involving microcontrollers, embedded firmware, and hardware architectures.

**Fault Injection & Side-Channel Analysis**  
Experimental fault injection, side-channel analysis, and security evaluation of embedded systems.

**Data Science & Experimental Intelligence**  
Statistical modelling, signal processing, Bayesian optimization, and data-driven experimental methods.

## Open Research

We aim to share research tools, technical findings, experimental methodologies, datasets, and AI models as our work develops.

Research projects and publications will be added as they become available.
